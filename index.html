A reader's key — post types explained
Experiment Log
noun  /ɪkˈsper.ɪ.mənt lɒɡ/

A structured account of a real-world productivity or systems test — including the problem, the method applied, and an honest record of what happened. Neither successes nor failures are edited out.

Field Notes
noun  /fiːld nəʊts/

Short, unpolished observations captured mid-experiment. What's holding, what's fraying, what surprised me. Less structured than an Experiment Log — more honest for it.
